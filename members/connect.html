⚡Connect in about 2 minutes

Add this URL in your Claude app, then sign in with your AI Founder University account.

https://aifounderuniversity.com/api/mcp
  1. Open claude.ai → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector.
  3. Paste the URL above and click Add.
  4. Click Connect and sign in with your AI Founder University account.

Claude is not showing the tools

Disconnect and reconnect the connector, or restart the Claude app. Claude re-reads what is available each time it reconnects.

Which apps work?

claude.ai on the web, the Claude desktop app, and Claude Code. Connect once and it follows you across them.

Is it safe?

Yes. It is read-only access to your own library: Claude can read your content but can never edit or delete anything, and it cannot see other members' data or anything about your payments. You can take the access away at any time by removing the connector in Claude.

Do I need a membership?

Yes — connecting to Claude comes with the AI Founder University membership, trials included. Anything you bought outright is always in your member area whatever happens to your subscription.
