      What exactly is a Claude routine?

      A routine is a Claude agent that runs on a schedule in Anthropic's cloud, connected to your tools. It runs on Anthropic's computers, not yours, so it works while your laptop is shut. Each of the 59 routines is a complete job: what to read, what to produce and where to deliver it. Set it up once and it runs on its schedule from then on.

      How do I install the routines?

      Go to claude.ai/code/routines, click New routine, paste the instructions, pick the schedule and keep the listed connectors. About 2 minutes each. No coding and nothing to install. If you see a box asking you to select a repository, you can leave it empty.

      Do I need a paid Claude account?

      Yes. Routines are on Claude Pro and Claude Max, not the free plan. Pro allows 5 runs a day and Max 15. That's runs, not routines: you can set up all 59 and switch on the ones you want. Each routine tells you how many runs it uses.

      Is it safe to let AI act inside my business?

      Most routines only read and report. The ones that write to customers or suppliers prepare drafts for you to send. Every routine keeps its channels, thresholds and time windows in one settings block you control.

      Can I change the routines?

      Yes. Every routine is fully editable. Change the schedule, the tone, the delivery channel or the output format.
