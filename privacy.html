Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains what personal data AI Founder University ("we", "us") collects when you visit aifounderuniversity.com or buy one of our products, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

AI Founder University is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at support@aifounderuniversity.com.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address and billing details when you buy, and anything you send us by email or through a contact form.
  • Payment information. Card details are entered on and handled by our third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage information. Pages viewed, referring site, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. See section 6.

3. Why we use it

  • To deliver the product you bought and provide support (performance of a contract).
  • To process payments and prevent fraud (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To send you service emails about your purchase (contract).
  • To send marketing emails where you have opted in or where permitted by law. You can unsubscribe from any marketing email at any time (consent / legitimate interests).
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it (legitimate interests).
  • To meet our tax, accounting and other legal obligations (legal obligation).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as far as they need it:

  • Payment processing
  • Product delivery and email
  • Website hosting
  • Analytics

These providers act on our instructions and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to establish or defend legal claims.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers are located outside your country. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK/EEA we rely on an adequacy decision or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Cookies

We use cookies that are strictly necessary for the site and checkout to work, and — where you consent — analytics and advertising cookies. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings; strictly necessary cookies cannot be turned off without breaking the site.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it. Order and transaction records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep them. Marketing contact data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Analytics data is kept in aggregate form only.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email support@aifounderuniversity.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Our site and products are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell you by email.

Questions about this page? Email support@aifounderuniversity.com.
