Before you join

Questions

Do I need my AI Roadmap first?

No. You only need an offer idea: what you sell and who it's for. The 10/90 Launch Brief walks you through the rest. If you already have your AI Roadmap, it names your offer, your buyer and your goal, so most of the brief is done: copy it across and Claude builds the offer from it.

How is this different from using Claude to make HTML for a funnel builder?

With a builder, Claude designs the page but you still paste it in, and the builder still runs your page and checkout. With the Funnel Engine, Claude works inside your own funnel code, builds around its checkout, bumps, upsells and dashboard, and you publish to your own hosting.

Is 30 minutes for real?

Once your engine's installed, yes. An offer goes from idea to live in about 30 minutes because Claude does the building. The first install takes longer, about an hour, and most of that is telling Claude about your offer.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to Claude in plain English. Claude writes the code.

Do I have to leave GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels or Kajabi?

No. Keep the tools you like. /Integrate connects your new funnel to them.

Does /Integrate work with my platform?

GHL, ClickFunnels, Kajabi, Systeme.io, Kit, MailerLite, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, or no email tool at all. Claude writes every email in your voice and sets up everything your tool allows directly, then clicks the rest in for you in your own Chrome while you watch. You do the logins and paste your own keys. ClickFunnels needs a paid Zapier plan to connect, and so do some Kajabi setups. Some free email plans can't send a sequence, so Claude checks yours first.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan, Chrome on a computer, a Stripe account, a domain, a hosting account (about $5 a month), and the email tool you already use. New to Stripe? Approval can take a few days, so open it today.

Is it safe?

Your keys stay in your own accounts and nothing runs through us. Claude never types your passwords or your keys.
